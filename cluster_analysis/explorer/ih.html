What a child of an XX and an XY profile could inherit. At every position each parent passes one of their two copies, each with a chance of one half, so for a variant both parents have a call at the child's odds are exact; how far they hold is how far the parents' calls do. Recessive means a child shows it only with a copy from both parents, dominant that one copy is enough. Modes come from HPO's disease annotations, variants from ClinVar, drug types from CPIC.
