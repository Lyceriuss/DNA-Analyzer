What XY's genes say about the nutrients XY needs, and which foods supply them. Per nutrient: where XY's genes put its blood level among Europeans (scores from the PGS Catalog and the GWAS Catalog), the intake to aim for (Nordic Nutrition Recommendations 2023, Livsmedelsverket), the richest foods (Livsmedelsverket's food database), the blood test that shows the actual level, and every finding with an evidence grade from A+ (ClinVar, expert-reviewed) to C (a single study). Genes only tilt a level; what XY eats, and the test, settle it.
