A theme gathers every library gene with evidence that it bears on one area of health, grouped by the mechanism it acts through: repair, growth signals, hormones, the supply of building blocks. Evidence comes from Gene Ontology, Reactome, UniProt and GWAS, and each gene shows why it is included. Module balance

Risk load (left) sums how far each risk SNP sits below the neutral score of 5; strength load (right) sums how far each strength SNP sits above it. Hover a bar for the genes.

Biomarker tilt

Standardised genetic score per lab test against European allele frequencies (0 = average). The band marks ±1. Only biomarkers with 3 or more scoreable SNPs are shown. It suggests what is worth measuring; it does not predict the value.

Gene interactions inside modules

Compound: both interacting genes are reduced, so effects may stack. Buffered: a reduced gene interacts with a strength gene, which may partly compensate. Score is STRING's interaction confidence (0–1).
