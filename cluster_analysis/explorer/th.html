A theme gathers every library gene with evidence that it bears on one area of health, grouped by the mechanism it acts through: repair, growth signals, hormones, the supply of building blocks. Evidence comes from Gene Ontology, Reactome, UniProt and GWAS, and each gene shows why it is included. Colours are the calls for the theme's person (HP; HJ for female fertility): blue more active, green typical function, red less active.
